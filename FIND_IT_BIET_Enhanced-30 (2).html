Terms & Conditions

  1. Do not mislead, prank, impersonate, or make false reports.
  2. Do not claim an item without verifying ownership.
  3. Meet in a safe public campus location and involve campus staff for valuable items.

  5. Contact details and photos must be handled responsibly.
  6. FIND IT is a campus coordination prototype; official staff should verify disputes and certificates.
